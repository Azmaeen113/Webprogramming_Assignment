Who We Are

FreeLanceHub KUET is a student-led club dedicated to empowering engineering students with freelancing skills, marketplace knowledge, and a strong professional network.

  • Weekly skill-building workshops on campus
  • Peer mentoring and buddy system for new members
  • Real client-style project simulations
  • Portfolio building sessions for Fiverr & Upwork

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join FreeLanceHub KUET?

Any KUET student interested in freelancing, digital skills, or earning online can apply through our join form.

Is membership free?

Yes. Club membership is free for KUET students. Some advanced bootcamps may have optional materials cost.

Do I need prior freelancing experience?

No. We welcome beginners and provide mentoring, workshops, and peer support from day one.

Where do club activities take place?

Most sessions are held at KUET campus — CSE Building, seminar halls, and innovation spaces.
